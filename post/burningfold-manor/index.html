Why Burningfold Manor is a gift for a photographer

Burningfold Manor is a Grade II listed Tudor manor house in Dunsfold, Surrey, near Godalming and right on the Sussex border. It has been one family's home for over twenty five years, set in 300 acres of fields and paddocks.

You get a timber-framed manor, a walled garden full of roses and wisteria, a lily pond and a marquee, and the whole estate is yours for the weekend.

The house has oak-panelled rooms, leaded windows and open fires. Outside, the Walled Garden sits inside old brick walls, a willow leans over the lily pond, and the Mulberry Lawn looks out over working farmland.

Every wedding here has exclusive use from Friday afternoon, so there is no other event to work around. It is an easy drive from our base in Berkshire, and about an hour from London.

The best photographs happen at Burningfold Manor

The Walled Garden. Roses and wisteria climb the old brick walls, and this is where outdoor ceremonies happen. The walls give even light all afternoon, and the gate frames your walk back down the aisle.

The Library. Oak panels, original beams, leaded windows and an open fireplace. If you marry indoors, this is the room, for around 60 to 70 guests. The window light is soft and warm.

The willow by the lily pond. A celebrant ceremony under the willow is the most relaxed setting on the estate. Later, the pond gives us reflections for portraits, with the marquee glowing behind it.

The courtyard and the Stable Bar. Drinks after the ceremony spill out here. Brick, gravel and a bar linked to the marquee, so the candid photographs of your guests keep coming.

The Mulberry Lawn and the oak tree. Wide open lawn, one big oak and 300 acres behind it. Room for the group photographs, and at golden hour the long light across the fields is where I take the portraits you frame.

The marquee at night. The permanent marquee looks over the lily pond and links to the party barn. Music and the bar run until midnight, and fireworks are permitted.

Burningfold Manor wedding photography: your questions

Is Burningfold Manor in Surrey or Sussex?

Burningfold Manor is in Surrey, in the village of Dunsfold near Godalming. The Sussex border is a short drive away, which is why you see both counties mentioned. The postcode is GU8 4PF.

How many guests can Burningfold Manor hold?

The permanent marquee seats up to 180 for dinner, and the Mulberry Lawn holds up to 180 for a ceremony. The Walled Garden takes well over 100, and the Library around 60 to 70. You can also bring your own marquee or stretch tent.

Can you get married outside at Burningfold Manor?

Yes. Outdoor ceremonies happen in the Walled Garden or on the Mulberry Lawn, and a celebrant can marry you under the willow beside the lily pond. Ask the venue which spaces hold the civil licence for your date.

Can you have a church wedding near Burningfold Manor?

The venue points couples to the churches at Dunsfold, Hascombe and Chiddingfold, all a short drive from the estate. I cover the church and then follow you back to the Manor for the reception.

What happens if it rains at Burningfold Manor?

The Library, the marquee and the Stable Bar all give us covered spots with good light, and a stretch tent can cover the Walled Garden. On a grey day the brick and oak look richer, not duller.

Can you stay at Burningfold Manor?

Yes. There is room for up to 32 guests across the Manor, the Coach House, Chapel Cottage and the Dolls House. A slow Sunday morning on the estate makes for relaxed photographs of you both before you leave.
